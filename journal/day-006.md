## Topics studied

- Clock and active-low reset: HCLK and HRESETn.
- Transfer readiness and response: HREADY and HRESP.
- Read/write direction and addressing: HWRITE and HADDR.
- Transfer size, burst type and transfer type: HSIZE, HBURST and HTRANS.
- Write and read data paths: HWDATA and HRDATA.
- Signal directions between the master, slave and interconnect.

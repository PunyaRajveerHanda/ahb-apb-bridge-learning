# Day 12 — AHB Revision and Introductory Master FSM Study

Date: 22 October 2026

## Today's work

Today's session focused mainly on revising earlier AHB concepts.
Alongside revision, I prepared two handwritten pages on an introductory
AHB master finite-state-machine example intended for Verilog implementation.

## Topics covered

- Revisited the distinction between address and data phases.
- Sketched the master interface and identified address, data,
  control and response signals.
- Studied the example's IDLE, write-address, write-data,
  read-address and read-data states.
- Reviewed word-sized transfers and read/write direction.
- Noted HREADY and burst-related conditions in the draft state transitions.
- Connected the earlier timing-diagram study with a state-machine view
  of transfer sequencing.

## Main takeaway

A timing diagram explains what happens across clock cycles.
An FSM describes how a controller moves between stages and generates
its required behavior.

Studying both views is helping me connect AHB protocol concepts
with the preparation needed for RTL design.

## Connection to the bridge project

Today's notes concern an introductory AHB master FSM.
They are conceptual study notes, not a completed master implementation
or the bridge controller itself.

Understanding master-generated traffic will support the later design
and verification of the full AMBA 2 AHB-to-APB3 bridge.

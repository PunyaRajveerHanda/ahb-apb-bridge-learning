# Day 8 — AHB multiple transfers, transfer types and sizes

Date: 18 October 2026

## Work completed

Continued the supplementary AHB lessons and prepared three handwritten pages covering overlapping transfers, wait states, transfer types and transfer-size encodings.

Studied a write-read-write sequence to understand how the data phase of one transfer overlaps the address phase of the next. Followed how a wait state extends the current data phase and the following address phase.

## Topics studied

- Address and data pairing across multiple transfers.
- HREADY-controlled completion and wait states.
- HTRANS encodings: IDLE, BUSY, NONSEQ and SEQ.
- Independent transfers versus transfers within a burst.
- HSIZE encodings and their corresponding data sizes.
- The requirement that transfer size must not exceed the data-bus width.
- Byte, halfword and word transfers on a 32-bit data bus.

- PSLVERR for reporting an unsuccessful transfer.

- An introduction to protection attributes and byte write strobes.

## Key understanding

The master drives PADDR, PSELx, PENABLE, PWRITE and PWDATA.
The slave returns PREADY, PRDATA and PSLVERR. PCLK and PRESETn
are inputs to both blocks.

The bridge implementation targets APB3. PPROT and PSTRB belong to
APB4 and later interfaces and are additional study topics.
APB uses SETUP and ACCESS phases: PENABLE is low during SETUP
and high during ACCESS. A read completes at a rising clock edge
with PSELx, PENABLE and PREADY high; PRDATA must be valid then.
PSLVERR is sampled at the completion of a transfer.

## Work completed

### WRAP8 address calculation

Worked through a WRAP8 burst using word transfers starting at `0x34`.

- Transfer size: four bytes per beat.
- Burst length: eight beats.
- Wrap-block size: `4 × 8 = 32 bytes`.
- Aligned address block: `0x20–0x3F`.

The address sequence is:

`0x34 → 0x38 → 0x3C → 0x20 → 0x24 → 0x28 → 0x2C → 0x30`

This example helped me understand how the address wraps within the aligned block instead of continuing to `0x40`.

### WRAP8 read timing

Drew the relationship between HCLK, HTRANS, HADDR, HWRITE, HBURST, HSIZE, HREADY and HRDATA.

Reviewed how successive address phases overlap with earlier data phases and how each returned data item corresponds to its address.

### Undefined-length INCR bursts

Studied how an INCR burst continues without announcing a fixed number of beats at the beginning.

Reviewed the roles of NONSEQ, SEQ and BUSY, how the master ends the burst, and the distinction between a pause and an additional data beat.

Also reinforced transfer-size alignment and the restriction against crossing a 1 KB boundary within an incrementing burst.

## Key takeaway

WRAP8 has a fixed number of beats and wraps within an aligned block. INCR has no predetermined beat count and continues through increasing addresses until the master ends the burst.

## Review and clarifications

- SPLIT belongs to legacy full AHB. Our planned AHB-Lite-to-APB3 bridge will not implement SPLIT, RETRY, HGRANT or HSPLIT.
- In the illustrated two-cycle SPLIT response, HRESP indicates SPLIT with HREADY LOW during T2–T3, followed by SPLIT with HREADY HIGH during T3–T4. The response ends at T4; the requested transfer has not successfully completed.
- The original manager cancels the following pipelined transfer by driving IDLE. HGRANT falling after T3 does not instantly transfer bus ownership while HREADY is LOW; the new manager drives its address after T4.
- Later, the subordinate uses HSPLIT to make the original manager eligible for arbitration again. After regaining the bus, that manager retries the interrupted transfer; the subordinate does not automatically deliver its result in the background.
- HRESP=OKAY during T4–T5 does not establish successful completion of the new manager's transfer at T5: the drawn HREADY is LOW at T5.
- Bursts and SPLIT responses are different concepts: a burst can itself receive a SPLIT response. The comparison table is a simplified example, not a rule that bursts always receive OKAY or retain ownership throughout.
- On handwritten page 3, change “maximum bus width of 32 bits” to “minimum bus width of 32 bits” when quoting the class slide's recommendation. The slide's expectation about 256 bits is historical guidance, not a universal upper limit. Our bridge baseline remains 32 bits.

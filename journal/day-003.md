## Day 3: AMBA AHB Architecture & Burst Transfers

* **System Design**: AMBA AHB connects multiple masters through a multiplexed interconnect rather than on-chip tristate buses. A central arbiter manages master access, while a decoder routes slave responses and read data.
* **Pipelined Transfers**: Implements pipelining where the address phase of a transfer runs concurrently with the data phase of the prior transfer, improving throughput when transfers are not stalled.
* **Burst Operations**: Groups related transfers into a sequence; the master supplies an address phase for every beat. Modes include SINGLE, undefined INCR, fixed incrementing (INCR4/8/16), and wrapping bursts (WRAP4/8/16).
* **WRAP4 Mechanics**: For a 4-beat word burst (4 × 4 = 16 bytes), boundaries align to 16-byte blocks. Starting at 0x38 (block range 0x30–0x3F), the address sequence increments and wraps: 0x38 → 0x3C → 0x30 → 0x34.
* **Timing & Handshaking**: Address phases drive HTRANS (NONSEQ for the initial beat, SEQ for subsequent beats) and HADDR. The data phase follows its address phase and can be extended by wait states. If the slave drives HREADY = 0, wait states insert to stretch the current data phase until ready.

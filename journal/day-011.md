## Worked timing example

Reviewed the following question and its supplied timing diagram:

Draw two consecutive independent SINGLE word writes:
1. Write 0x11 to address 0x1000.
2. Write 0x22 to address 0x1004.

Assume the master owns the bus, no wait states and OKAY responses.
Identify the address phases, data phases and completion edges.

![Two independent AHB word writes](../notes/day-011-two-writes.png)

From reviewing the example, I understood that:
- The second address phase overlaps the first write's data phase.
- HADDR and HWDATA can describe different transfers in the same cycle.
- Both independent SINGLE transfers begin with NONSEQ.
- The first write completes at T2 and the second at T3.
- IDLE prevents a new request but does not cancel the preceding data phase.

Today I focused on understanding the worked example.
I will redraw it independently in the next session.

## Worked address examples

For four-byte transfers in a WRAP4 burst, the block size is 16 bytes.

- Starting at 0x00: 0x00, 0x04, 0x08, 0x0C.
- Starting at 0x04: 0x04, 0x08, 0x0C, 0x00.
- Starting at 0x14: 0x14, 0x18, 0x1C, 0x10.

The corresponding block bases are 0x00, 0x00 and 0x10.
The block-size calculation uses the decoded bytes per transfer and
beat count, rather than multiplying the raw HSIZE and HBURST encodings.

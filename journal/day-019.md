# Day 19 — APB Slave Error Response

**Date:** 31 October 2026

## What I learned

Today I studied slave error responses and revised the APB operating states.

- PSLVERR is driven by the APB slave and received by the master/bridge. It is available in APB3 and later revisions.
- A transfer completes on a rising PCLK edge when PSEL, PENABLE and PREADY are all HIGH. PSLVERR is sampled at that completion: HIGH indicates an error and LOW indicates success.
- SETUP has PSEL = 1 and PENABLE = 0; ACCESS has both HIGH. PWRITE selects write (1) or read (0).
- PREADY = 0 extends ACCESS. Address, control and write data remain stable during waits.
- A write can complete with an error; that response does not guarantee the peripheral state was unchanged.
- A read can complete with an error, so returned PRDATA must not be assumed to contain valid requested data.
- After completion, another request takes the FSM to SETUP; otherwise it returns to IDLE. Consecutive transfers still require SETUP.

## Notes review

The handwritten statement about asserting PSEL and PENABLE describes ACCESS. A transfer first enters SETUP with PENABLE LOW. PSLVERR is meaningful at completion with PSEL, PENABLE and PREADY all HIGH, rather than whenever PREADY alone is HIGH.
